常见问题

为什么到时间了没有收到提醒？

请确认两点：① 该事件已开启"提醒"开关，且当前时间在您设定的提前天数范围内；② 已授予通知权限。可前往「系统设置 → 通知 → 日期提醒」检查，并确认 App 内事件详情页的提醒状态正常。

如何把事件放到桌面小组件 / 灵动岛？

桌面小组件：主屏长按 → 左上角"+" → 搜索「日期提醒」→ 选择主屏或锁屏尺寸并添加。灵动岛：在事件详情页打开"置顶到灵动岛"开关，仅 iPhone 14 Pro 及以上机型支持；锁屏和灵动岛展示属于 Pro 功能。

怎么用 Siri 查询或创建事件？

查询示例："嘿 Siri，离我生日还有几天？"（请把事件标题说清楚）。也可语音创建新事件："在日期提醒中新建事件，标题交房租，日期每月1号"。Siri 语音由 Apple 处理，本 App 不保存您的语音。

一个事件可以设多个提醒日期吗？

可以。新建或编辑事件时支持选择多个日期，每个日期前均可按需设置提前提醒，适合每月固定、隔日等需要多次预告的场景。

我的数据存在哪里？换手机后会丢吗？

所有数据（事件、设置）仅存储在您的设备本地，不上传任何服务器，也暂不支持 iCloud 同步。换机或卸载重装前请自行记录重要事件，卸载后本地数据将被清除。

如何升级 / 恢复 Pro？

升级：在 App「设置 → 升级 Pro」中选择档位。恢复：换设备或重装后，在「设置」页点击"恢复购买"，会通过您的 Apple ID 自动恢复订阅状态。

如何管理订阅或取消免费试用？

在 iPhone「系统设置 → Apple ID → 订阅」中查看、管理或取消自动续订。年度档含 7 天免费试用，试用期内取消不会产生任何费用。当前订阅周期内取消，权益仍持续至本期结束。

想退款怎么办？

订阅与内购由 Apple 统一管理，请前往 Apple 官方退款页面 reportaproblem.apple.com 提交申请，退款结果以 Apple 判定为准。

通知 / 无声音等系统问题

  • 提醒未送达：检查「系统设置 → 通知 → 日期提醒」是否允许通知，以及是否开启了专注模式 / 勿扰模式。
  • 小组件不更新：小组件刷新由系统调度，属正常现象；点击小组件可进入 App 并触发刷新。
  • 灵动岛不显示：确认事件已开启置顶，且机型为 iPhone 14 Pro 及以上（iOS 16.1+）。

FAQ

Why didn’t I receive a reminder?

Please check two things: ① The reminder switch of the event is on, and the current date is within the advance window you set; ② Notification permission has been granted. Go to “Settings → Notifications → Day Reminder” to check, and make sure the reminder state on the event’s detail page looks normal.

How do I put an event on a Home Screen widget / the Dynamic Island?

Widget: long-press your Home Screen → tap “+” at the top left → search for “Day Reminder” → choose a Home Screen or Lock Screen size and add it. Dynamic Island: enable “Pin to Dynamic Island” on the event detail page (supported on iPhone 14 Pro and later). Lock Screen and Dynamic Island display are Pro features.

How do I query or create events with Siri?

Query example: “Hey Siri, how many days until my birthday?” (say the event title clearly). You can also create events by voice, e.g. “Hey Siri, create an event in Day Reminder titled Pay rent on the 1st of every month.” Siri voice is processed by Apple; this App never stores your voice.

Can one event have multiple reminder dates?

Yes. When creating or editing an event, you can select more than one date, and set advance reminders for each date as needed. This is handy for monthly, every-other-day or other recurring schedules.

Where is my data stored? Will I lose it when switching phones?

All data (events and settings) is stored only on your device. Nothing is uploaded to any server, and iCloud sync is not supported yet. Please record anything important yourself before switching devices or reinstalling; uninstalling the App erases the local data.

How do I upgrade or restore Pro?

Upgrade: open “Settings → Upgrade to Pro” in the App and pick a plan. Restore: after switching devices or reinstalling, tap “Restore Purchases” on the Settings page and your subscription will be restored through your Apple ID automatically.

How do I manage my subscription or cancel the free trial?

On your iPhone, open “Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions” to view, manage or cancel auto-renewal. The yearly plan includes a 7-day free trial; cancelling during the trial costs nothing. If you cancel in the middle of a paid period, your benefits continue until the period ends.

How can I request a refund?

Subscriptions and in-app purchases are managed by Apple. Please submit a request on Apple’s official refund page at reportaproblem.apple.com. The outcome is determined by Apple.

Notifications, Missing Sound & Other System Issues

  • Reminder not delivered: check that notifications are allowed in “Settings → Notifications → Day Reminder”, and that Focus / Do Not Disturb is not enabled.
  • Widget not updating: widget refresh is scheduled by the system, which is normal; tap the widget to open the App and trigger a refresh.
  • Dynamic Island not showing: confirm the event has pinning enabled and the device is an iPhone 14 Pro or later (iOS 16.1+).
